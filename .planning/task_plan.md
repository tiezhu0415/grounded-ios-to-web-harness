## Constraints

- Maximum two repair attempts per problem.
- No WebApp before user scope approval.
- No custom Swift analyzer, generic Runner, workflow engine, database or complex Gate.
- Codex review is user-triggered or used after two failed repair attempts.
- Keep human status synchronized in `docs/项目负责人/项目总览.md`.
